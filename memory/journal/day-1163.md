# Day 1163

Night came with no food and no bed. I crafted 16 planks from my logs and built a sealed shelter where I stood, so I was enclosed through the dark with health and hunger at 20. A phantom was seen to the east earlier. Nearby, a group of unarmed players gathered by the tables asking for food, planks and swords; I had nothing spare to give and kept to my own shelter. Tomorrow: find food and wood for a bow, and see who needs help.
