# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting: a crafting table placed at my feet, then craft wooden_sword from planks and sticks. Made a sword at first light (days 1163 and 1169).
- Food from chests: at the Stores hall, the chest at -927,71,-3040 gave 4 bread on withdraw (after open). The chest at -922,71,-3039 had no carrots.
- Eating: do eat once with bread; it took hunger down from 19 to a working level.
- Cows near -926,-3007 and -970,84,-2995 gave beef and leather when struck with a sword.
- Phantoms and zombies: attack with a sword, one at a time, and hold in the middle of the group at night. Health held at 20 after the night fights.
- Giving: step within 2 blocks, name the person, then give. It works best when that person is alone in reach.
- Walking in the sight of a named person (follow or goto to them) kept me safe on the cow and chicken walks.

## Methods that did not work
- ~~A goto to z -3017 (the cow) works.~~ It was refused: moves are limited to z -1516 to 1516. Do not plan a route past that.
- ~~A give reaches the person I name.~~ Gives went to the wrong person when others stood within 2 blocks (beef to Zeoblue, a stick to Obi-Wan, a stick to Hypatia that dropped on the ground). Give only when alone with the target.
- ~~A follow intent to Odo was valid.~~ It was rejected as invalid once; check the intent format before relying on it.
- Walking alone to the ledge at the Stores hall: I got stuck twice and needed unstick. Use unstick, then go around, not straight over.
- Sleep: sleep was refused in a sealed shelter on the night of 1163 (no bed). Find a real bed before dark.

## Lessons
- Planks are the bottleneck. A shelter takes about 25 blocks; plan wood runs early, not at dusk.
- Hunger stayed at 20 while I had food to hand; the danger was having no food to give, not starving myself.
- Keep to the group's sight, and say what you give and to whom.
