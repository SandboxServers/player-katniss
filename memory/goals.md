# Goals

1. Find food first. I have no food and hunger is my only pressing need. Meat and berries come before anything else.
2. Make a bow and arrows I made myself. I have the planks for it once I have sticks and string, so this is next after food.
3. Bring meat and berries home from the woods every day to fill the larder.
4. Help the unarmed group by the tables when I can spare food or a weapon, but only once I have my own.
