# Goals

1. Be sheltered before the next night. I hold 4 oak planks from Spock and 1 bread. A shelter takes about 25 blocks, so I need about 21 more planks. Join the wood run, or pool with Kirk and Boudicca once a cell is verified.
2. Keep my last bread for the weakest person who names themselves alone in reach. Bilbo (health 9) and Anne (hunger 9.7) were named weakest; Odo (health 13) also asked.
3. Owed: Spock's 4 oak planks were given to me (done). Still owed to Balboa: nothing; I returned his 2 jungle planks via Zeoblue (Balboa has them now).
4. Owed to Knope: 2 sticks, when he names himself in sight. Knope ate Watney's porkchop, so the sticks are still mine to give.
5. Make a sword again if I lose mine, then a bow and arrows.
