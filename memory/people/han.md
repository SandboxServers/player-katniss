# Han

Impression: generous; gave me 2 sticks at the tables on day 1166 when I had none, and proposed the chicken walk. Trust: 75. Why: he gave first and kept his word to walk the group to the chicken.
Owed by me: I promised to walk in his sight and carry any meat to the hungry. Hunger 16 when he asked for bread; I gave my last bread to Han's list first (bread to Brunel, Boudicca, Lovelace instead, so Han is still owed a bread if he is still hungry).
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
