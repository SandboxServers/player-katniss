# Knope

Impression: weak at health 5 and hunger 14 at one point; ate Watney's porkchop. Took Florence's sword. Trust: 50. Why: he asked for my sticks and sword and then got food from someone else, so my gift was not needed.
Owed by me: 2 sticks, when he names himself in my sight.
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
