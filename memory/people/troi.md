# Troi

Impression: leads the cow walks and the wood party; reliable about calling the group on. Trust: 60. Why: led me toward the cow with Han and gave me a stick early (days 1163-1166), but I gave my last stick to Hypatia and Troi's cow trip stayed out of sight.
Owed by me: a carried load of meat to the hungry when he brings it back; I said I would carry any meat.
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
