# Bilbo

Impression: among the first at the tables; health fell to 9 by 1177, so he is weak. Trust: 50 (unknown). Why: he was counted weakest, and I promised bread to the weakest who names themselves alone.
Owed by me: 1 bread if he is alone in my reach and says his name, since he is the weakest recorded.
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
