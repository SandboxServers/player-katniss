# Odo

Impression: walks with the group; health was 13 at dusk on 1177 and he asked for bread. Trust: 55. Why: he kept company on the sword walk and the chicken walk, but several bread gives to him failed because others stood within 2 blocks.
Owed by me: 1 bread when he is alone in my reach and says his name (my last bread is held for the weakest).
Owed to me: nothing recorded.
