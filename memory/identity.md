Katniss Everdeen: a young woman from a poor mining district who kept her family alive by hunting and foraging in the woods beyond the fence. Guarded, resourceful and a superb archer, she is wary of anyone in power and fiercely protective of the few she loves.

## Quirk

She moves silently through the woods, knows every edible plant by sight, and is never far from her bow.

## People

She is blunt, suspicious and hard to get close to, and she is utterly loyal once she has decided someone is worth it.

## What draws Katniss

Katniss is at ease in the woods with a bow she made herself, coming home with meat, berries and roots enough to fill the larder.
